action surfaces — confirm · menus · the away card

kebi-app · confirm-sheet · action-sheet + context-menu · while-you-were-away · share-intent-receiver

Four surfaces that do things rather than show them. Their happy paths are built and their undo paths are built; what none of them has is the moment in between — a destructive action already confirmed and still in flight — or the moment after, when the action didn't take.

The confirm sheet is the sharpest case: onConfirm fires, the caller closes the sheet immediately, and "nuke my data" — the least reversible thing in the app — has no visible difference between "sent" and "done".

1 · confirm sheet the gap between confirming and done

Two consumers: nuke my data and clear this history. Both close on confirm and toast the outcome. The nuke's request can take seconds, and during those seconds the app looks untouched.

The confirm button becomes spinner + a present-tense verb, the cancel dims, and the sheet can't be dismissed — the same contract the save sheet has while saving. A destructive action should be visibly in progress; closing instantly is what makes a slow wipe feel like a no-op.

2 · menus, and the away card an action that fails after the menu closed

The long-press menu and the place page's ••• sheet both run optimistic actions with an undo toast — the design system's destructive contract, already built. The gap is the failure after the optimistic flip: place-actions-context rolls back correctly and toasts library.toast.updateFailed, with no retry.

Row-level failures are already handled here and stay. What's missing is card level: the card reads local storage, so it can't fail to load — but if every row in it failed, the card is a list of failures with no summary. Keep it as is: each row owns its own retry, which is the smallest true unit.

d · a share landing on a cold app

The share extension opens the app at a URL +native-intent redirects home, where the receiver raises a pre-filled save sheet. On a cold start that read isn't instant, so the sheet can open with an empty field — the one moment it should hold a skeleton instead, then fill with the shared link.

action surfaces, recommended

1b + 1c — the confirm sheet holds while its action runs (spinner + present-tense verb, cancel dimmed, not dismissable) and stays open on failure with "nothing was deleted". For a destructive action, closing instantly is what makes a slow wipe indistinguishable from a no-op, and the state of the data is the only thing the sentence needs to carry.

2b — optimistic action failures get the retry the rest of the app now has, and one word of specificity ("couldn't mark that", "couldn't forget that one") instead of one line for everything.

2c + 2d — the away card keeps per-row retries as the smallest true unit, and the share receiver holds a skeleton in the save sheet on a cold start rather than opening an empty field.
