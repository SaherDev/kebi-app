The rule the swap follows

  • A row lists the level below. Not "the same list, shorter" — the section's contents change with scope while its layout does not. One row component, two payloads.
  • The last rung is the only one that lists places. Everything above it lists areas, so drilling down is the same gesture until it stops.
  • The count is the drill-down promise. "Bali 8" means eight of your saves are somewhere under that key — it is what makes an area row worth tapping instead of a dead label.
  • The summary carries the scope. The profiler is asked at the level of the key, so a country reads as orientation and a neighbourhood reads as a night out. Same field, different altitude.

Still open

  • Levels are not uniform. id/bali is an island, id/jakarta is a city. One "region" label covers both, which is honest but loose.
  • Areas with no saves. At country scope "where you've saved" hides every region you have not been to, so the screen cannot tell you Indonesia has a Flores. That is the cut discovery section showing up again, one rung higher.
  • Sideways movement is gone. With nearby dropped, the only way out of an area is up the breadcrumb or down a row — you cannot get from Canggu to Seminyak without going through Bali.
