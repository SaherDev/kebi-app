What the shape has to say

  • "I am not a venue" — so a tap is not a place card. Every option except 1 says this.
  • "I am not less than a venue" — the area is the turn's scope, and it leads the rail. Outline and pill both quietly say secondary; fill, size, and the edge rule do not.
  • "I hold things" — only 4 and 5 actually communicate that a tap opens a list rather than a detail. The rest rely on the emoji.

Locked: 1 · identical

  • One chip, two kinds. An area takes the venue chip unchanged. The emoji is the whole distinction, which is consistent with how areas are drawn everywhere else in the app.
  • Order: mention order, areas included. With identical chips there is no visual grouping to preserve, and the answer names its scope first anyway ("a great night to be in canggu"), so first-mention already puts the area at the head of the rail in the common case. Areas-first would only change turns where the area is named late — and there it would contradict the sentence.
  • The tap must be forgiving. Nothing on the chip warns that this one opens a list rather than a place card, so the area screen has to read as an obvious destination on arrival — which is what the breadcrumb, the level chip, and the summary hero are for.

Constant in all six

  • The venue chip does not change — whatever is chosen is additive.
  • The rail label is mentioned, not places — a mixed rail breaks the old word.
  • Icons come from entity.icon with the per-kind fallback that already exists (CHAT_ENTITY_FALLBACK_ICON) — no new data for any of these.
