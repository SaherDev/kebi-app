home — loading, empty, error

kebi-app · apps/mobile/src/app/index.tsx · applies the kernel (skeleton · ghost + add-row · error over frozen skeleton)

Home is four surfaces on three lifecycles: the greeting + chips (useHome, the expensive taste-dependent read), what you wanted (useIntents), while you were away (only ever renders when something landed), and your stash (useStash). They arrive independently and none blocks the others — so home never has one state, it has a mix. Today all four just pop in as they resolve, and a failed fetch renders nothing at all: the section silently doesn't exist.

Three decisions here: which sections may shimmer, what a brand-new user sees, and where a failed section puts its error.

1 · cold open which sections are allowed to shimmer?

The trap: a skeleton is a promise that something is coming. "what you wanted" and "your stash" can legitimately resolve to nothing — so shimmering them means a brand-new user watches two sections appear, pulse, and then vanish. Only the greeting and chips are guaranteed to exist (the endpoint fails open with fallback chips).

3 · a section fails home has three fetches — who reports the failure?

Home must never be blocked by a failed fetch: the greeting endpoint already fails open (fallback chips), so the only real failures are the stash and recall reads. Today both fail silently — the section just isn't there, indistinguishable from being empty. That's the actual bug: a user with 40 saves sees the same home as a user with none.

home, decided

1c — shimmer only the greeting, chips and location line. Never skeleton a section that is allowed to resolve to nothing, or day-one users watch phantom content evaporate.

2b — one ghost stash group with the + row. It's the only thing on home that teaches where saves live, it costs one card, and it self-destructs after the first save. "what you wanted" stays hidden when empty — nothing to recall means nothing to draw.

3b + 3c — each section owns its failure (frozen skeleton + retry line inside its own card), offline shows in the location line, and a failed refresh is a toast over intact content. Home itself is never blocked: the greeting and chips survive any backend failure, because asking kebi a question is the one thing home must always be able to do.
