place — loading, empty, error

kebi-app · apps/mobile/src/app/place.tsx · ADR-151 · applies the kernel

The place page is reached two ways, and they are not the same problem. From a library card, a home stash row or a chat card, the caller placeDetail.set(view)s first — the screen already holds the name, emoji, pills and detail line and paints instantly, then refreshes. From a deep link, the share extension, or a back-stack entry, it opens cold with only an id.

Today both go through the same branch: status !== 'ready' → centred spinner, or one grey line for any failure with no retry. The seeded case throws away data it already has.

1 · opening a place seeded and cold are different waits

Nine out of ten opens are seeded. Showing a spinner over data we're already holding is the single biggest wasted opportunity on this screen.

"been there", "i like this one", a note edit, a forget — all optimistic per the design system. The pill flips immediately; if the write fails it flips back and a toast carries the retry. No section of the page is ever replaced by an action's failure. Already the rule — listed so place's set is complete.

place, decided

1b + 1c — split the two entrances. Seeded: paint everything the card already knew (eyebrow, emoji + name, pill, source, top pill) and shimmer only what it never carried. Cold: full skeleton including the title, and no top pill until we know whether the place is saved.

2a + 2b — keep the bare-place line, and add nothing else. An individual tag section that has no content disappears; it never draws a header with "nothing here yet" under it.

3a–3d — four failures, four different answers: a seeded refresh is a toast over live content, a cold open freezes its skeleton under a retry, a gone place gets a plain explanation and no retry, and an optimistic action that fails reverts and toasts. The one thing they share is that none of them is a grey sentence you can't act on.
