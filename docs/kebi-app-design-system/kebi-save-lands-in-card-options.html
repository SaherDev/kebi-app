in-app saves, same card

kebi-app · the save sheet writes a row too — and nothing else changes

You hit save it in the app and the result lands in the same card a shared link does. That is the whole change. What is left to decide is small: what the section is called now that half its rows did not happen while you were away, and what a typed place looks like as a row, since it has no link and no app to name.

Not changing: the save sheet still waits its grace window and still relaxes to "we'll finish in the background"; the success and failure toasts stay exactly as they are; saved places still appear in your stash the moment they resolve. The card gains a row, nothing loses anything.

what I would do

1 · a and b as drawn. A pasted link is already a shared row, so it just is one — no badge distinguishing "saved in app" from "shared in", because the user knows which they did. A typed place gets the pencil it already has elsewhere in the app, its own words as the name, and no second line.

2 · a — "just saved" on the card. It is the only one that reads as a receipt rather than a catalogue, and it cannot be mistaken for the stash sitting under it.

The one wrinkle: the /shares screen behind "show all" is a week of history, so "just saved" is the wrong title there — I would call that screen "recent saves" and leave the card as "just saved". Two phrases, each true where it sits. If you would rather have one word everywhere, that word is c.
