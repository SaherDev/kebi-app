design 1 — a square, centred

kebi-app · ios SavetoKebi · TikTok stays visible behind it

A rounded square in the middle of the screen, over TikTok, for 0.6 seconds. This is the shape iOS itself uses for exactly this job — the volume HUD, the AirDrop badge, the silent-mode switch — a thing that appears, says one thing, and leaves without asking anything of you.

It also solves the problem with the bottom card: a wide bar reads as interface, while a small centred square reads as a moment. Nothing here is tappable.

the square is the right call

Shape does the work here. The bottom card failed for a reason that was never about size: edge-to-edge furniture implies permanence. A centred rounded square is the platform's own vocabulary for "temporary, informational, not your problem" — the user has been trained on it by every volume change they have ever made.

148pt over 132. The url is the single thing this moment can prove, and it costs 16pt. After this instant nothing is visible until the app is next opened, so it is the cheapest trust in the whole flow.
