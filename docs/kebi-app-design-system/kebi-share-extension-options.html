the share extension moment

kebi-app · ios SavetoKebi · what shows over TikTok for ~1s

iOS presents the share extension's view controller whether or not it draws anything — that is the black box on the device right now. It is not a bug and it cannot be skipped; the only question is what fills it between "tapped Save to Kebi" and completeRequest.

This reopens a decision we already made. We rejected a confirmation because it would cost the user time — but that presentation happens regardless, so the time is already spent. A blank box spends it and says nothing.

Wording is the constraint. At this instant nothing is saved: we hold a link and have handed it to iOS. Anything claiming success is a lie we may have to take back in the "while you were away" card, which is the one surface that must stay trustworthy.

why a

The box is not optional, so the only real choice is what it says. a fills the exact space iOS already reserves, at the exact duration it already takes — the cost is zero because it is already being paid.

a is also the only option that carries the url. That line is what makes this a receipt rather than a reassurance: you can see Kebi took the video you meant, which is the one thing you cannot check later without opening the app.

"kebi's got it" is load-bearing. A checkmark and "saved to kebi" would be a claim we cannot back — the link may still turn out to be unsupported, and the failure surfaces in "while you were away". Promising success here and retracting it there is exactly how that card stops being believed.

c stays worth revisiting if the panel ever feels heavy in daily use — it is the same copy in a lighter frame, and the url is the only thing lost.
