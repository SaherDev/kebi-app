the share moment

kebi-app · ios SavetoKebi · two candidates, same words, same 0.6s

You tap Save to Kebi in TikTok. The link is handed to iOS in about 50 ms — but iOS then shows the extension's own screen for a moment. This is that moment, and the only question is whether TikTok stays visible behind it.

the constraint

iOS presents a share extension in a sheet locked to full height — an Apple engineer confirmed the size cannot be changed. Whether that sheet can be made see-through is the open question: clearing the view, its superview and the host window did not work, and the remaining option (NSExtensionActionWantsFullScreenPresentation) is on the phone now, untested.

Design 1 needs transparency. Design 2 does not. That is the whole difference.

how this gets decided

One share from TikTok. The build on the phone right now is design 1 with the full-screen-presentation flag — the last untried way to get a transparent background.

TikTok visible behind the card → design 1 works, it is the answer, and the earlier full-screen builds were fighting the wrong presentation mode.
Black screen → transparency is not possible on iOS 26, and design 2 goes back in immediately. There is nothing further to try: the sheet cannot be resized, and this flag was the only remaining option Apple names.

The copy does not change either way. saving this…, never "saved" — at that instant we hold a link and have handed it to iOS. A success claim here would have to be taken back in "while you were away" when a link turns out unsupported, and that surface is the only trust a silent flow has.
