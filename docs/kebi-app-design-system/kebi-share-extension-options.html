the share extension second

kebi-app · ios SavetoKebi · supersedes the earlier version of this page

Tapping Save to Kebi in TikTok hands the link to iOS in about 50 ms. Everything on this page is about the second after that — which iOS spends showing the extension's own screen, whether we want it or not.

what changed since the last version of this page

The earlier design drew a small card over a visible TikTok. That is not achievable. iOS presents a share extension full-screen in its own window and will not let it be transparent — verified on iOS 26.6, after clearing the view, its superview, and the host window. The host app is blacked out for the whole duration regardless of what we draw.

So the real choice was never "card or nothing". It is what fills an unavoidable full-screen second, and how short we can make it.

the unavoidable second 4 options

what fills it

Real proportions. The thin strip at the top is the sliver of the host app iOS leaves visible — the only reminder you never actually left TikTok. Nothing here is tappable; it dismisses itself. Duration is part of each design, not a setting applied afterwards.

what I think

b. The second is not negotiable, so the only question is whether it looks deliberate. a is the old design surviving into a context it was not drawn for — a small card marooned in a full black screen reads as a mistake, because it is one: it was designed to sit over something.

Shorter is not automatically better. c chases the original promise, but a screen that appears and vanishes in a third of a second is exactly what you flagged as "weird" at the start. Below roughly half a second this stops reading as feedback and starts reading as a flicker.

Keep the url. It is the only thing in the whole flow that shows Kebi caught the video you meant — everything after this point is invisible until you next open the app. d is right that it matters, but wrong to make it the hero: a shortlink is not a place name.

The copy stays honest either way. saving this…, never "saved" — at this instant we hold a link and have handed it to iOS. A claim here has to be retracted in "while you were away" when a link turns out unsupported, and that card is the only trust a silent flow has.
