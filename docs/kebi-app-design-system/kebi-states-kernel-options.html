empty, loading, error — the kernel

kebi-app · apps/mobile · decide once here, then every page file applies it

Every mobile screen needs three states we never designed: nothing yet, on its way, and it broke. Today most screens show a <Spinner /> — which design-system.md explicitly bans ("spinners are for windows 95") — and a couple show a bare line of muted text.

Rather than invent a different answer on fourteen screens, this page picks the three patterns once. Pick one option per section below and the per-page files that follow are just those patterns cut to each screen's real content shape.

1 · loading which shape does "on its way" take?

The rule from design-system.md holds either way: nothing under 1s, and the splash is app-boot only. This is about what fills the gap on a normal fetch (library, area, place, notes, shares).

2 · empty how loud is "nothing here yet"?

Two kinds of empty, and they must not look the same. Cold empty — you have never had anything here (a first-run stash, no notes yet) — is an invitation. Filtered empty — a search or an area with no match — is a dead end you back out of. Pick the treatment for cold empty; filtered empty is settled at the end.

2b · the empty state's action the filled slab is too loud — what replaces it?

A filled black button is the heaviest object the system has, and here it lands in the softest composition on the screen — faded ghost rows, muted caption. It also spends the screen's one primary button on a screen where the top-bar bookmark is already the save trigger. Same ghost preview in all four, only the action changes.

3 · error where does the failure live?

design-system.md already fixes the tiers: recoverable → toast with retry, blocking → full-screen with an action, catastrophic → back to splash. What it doesn't fix is the recoverable case when there is nothing on screen to fall back to — a first load that failed. That's the choice here.

today, on library only

a · centred block + retry

your stash
couldn't load your places
check your connection and try again.

Honest and impossible to miss. The cost is that a two-second network blip takes the whole screen hostage, and the retry is a full round trip from an empty page.

recommended

b · skeleton holds, error on top

your stash
couldn't load your places. nothing lost — try again.

The skeleton stops shimmering and dims, and the failure sits above it as a row you can retry without leaving. The screen keeps its shape, so a retry that works just fills in — no second layout jump. One line, one action, no code, per the voice rules.

what I'd pick

1b · skeleton in the content's shape, 2c · ghost preview + one action, and 3b · skeleton holds, error on top.

They're one idea, not three: the screen keeps its shape no matter what's happening to the data. The same row geometry shimmers while loading, sits pale and captioned when there's nothing to show, and dims under a retry line when the fetch fails. Nothing jumps, nothing is a blank page, and the user always sees the shape of what they're waiting for.

For the empty state's action (2b): the ghost's last row is the action. A filled button was the wrong instinct — it's the heaviest ink in the system landing on the emptiest screen, and it spends the one primary button on a screen whose top bar already has a save trigger. Making the action the next row in the list keeps the whole composition one object: two rows that were, one row that could be. It also works on every screen, including the ones with no bookmark in their pill.
